$TTL 3600
@        IN  MX  10  mx.edu.as.
mx       IN  A   YOUR_SERVER_IPV4
@        IN  TXT "v=spf1 mx a:mx.edu.as ~all"
_dmarc   IN  TXT "v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:postmaster@edu.as"

=== NOTES ===
1. Replace YOUR_SERVER_IPV4 with the public IPv4 of the host that runs PersistMail SMTP on port 25.
2. Do NOT orange-cloud (proxy) the mx A record — mail requires a raw IP.
3. Local/dev SMTP stays on :2525. Production MX requires :25 on mx.edu.as.
4. Set MAIL_IPV4 in .env to stamp the real IP into GET /api/dns/edu.as
5. Vercel cannot terminate SMTP. Point MX at a VPS / always-on host, not vercel.app.
